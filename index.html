    Yep, that's me chillin' on a boat with one of my boys. No, unfortunately I'm not really in a Nintendo DS game. While I'd love to say I started coding as a kid, my journey into tech actually began with studying Biology, fascinated by how doctors and engineers bridge science and technology to solve real-world medical problems.


    I launched my career as a Manufacturing Scientist at an FDA-regulated lab, then transitioned to a Biomedical Technician role during the COVID-19 pandemic. There, I trained over 10 technicians, led ISO 13485 certification audits, and managed thousands of critical equipment while troubleshooting complex systems. This role deepened my understanding of electrical, mechanical, and pneumatic systems.


    Despite having no previous coding experience, I had a weird inkling that there was going to be a shift in the importance of software soon. After taking a leap of faith, I enrolled in DevMountain's web development program where I gained hands-on experience working with JavaScript and React. Since completing the course, I've been staying up to date with the latest technologies and frameworks. Pushing the boundaries on how much STEM info I can fit in my head, I'm always excited to learn and challenge myself. Please reach out if you think I might be a good candidate for your next team.
